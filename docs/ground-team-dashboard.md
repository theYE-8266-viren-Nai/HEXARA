# Ground health team preview

The **Ground health team** entry on the start page opens a synthetic training console. It groups crew by mission and shows only profiles with all three conditions: `stage = active`, `assignment = onboard`, and a mission marked ongoing whose dates include today. The demo includes invented missions, people, readings, and one crew-reported urgent concern. Planned missions, returned crew, Future travelers, and retired astronauts are excluded.

The dashboard supports mission selection, compact crew selectors, crew detail, six synthetic trends for every onboard astronaut, source and data-quality context, and a collapsible handoff checklist. A data-gap crew member still has historical charts, but their latest readings are explicitly unavailable. Staff can edit and copy a draft for an **approved medical communications channel**. The copy action does not transmit a message. If `VITE_GROUND_COMMS_URL` is configured with an HTTPS URL, a link opens that portal without including crew data in the URL. No acknowledgment, medical decision, or handoff is recorded by OrbitWell.

This public preview does **not** read astronaut accounts or live health data, and it does not provide ground-team authorization. Real team access would require administrator-assigned mission membership, authenticated team roles, mission-scoped row-level security, an approved telemetry ingestion path with source timestamps and quality, an audited communications integration, and a tested emergency operations procedure. Do not expose existing owner-only `astronaut_profiles` or private check-ins to this preview.

The contact wording follows NASA's public descriptions of [flight surgeons and crew medical officers](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/faq-the-real-story-about-astronaut-health-care-in-space/) and [medical operations coordination](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/ochmo-tb-044-medical-operations.pdf). The demo intentionally provides no treatment instructions or emergency dispatch capability.
